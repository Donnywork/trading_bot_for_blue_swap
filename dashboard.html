Strategy comparison

Every strategy, replayed on the recorded prices with your current take profit, stop loss, time stop, guard and cooldown (each with its own saved numbers). It is a paper replay: fills at the recorded price minus about 0.75% per round trip, the cost the live trades measured. The prices are the ones the settings were tuned on, so use it to rank ideas, not to forecast.

Extra exit: time stop (off by default)

Sells whatever is held once it has been open this long, at the market price, if neither the take profit nor the stop loss has done it. A backtest on 43 hours of recorded prices found 30 minutes best of 15 to 60, so 30 is the default, but it is a starting point, not a proven value. Ticking it also applies to a position that is already open: one older than this is sold on the next tick.

Working it out…

The bot reads these before every tick (about every 30 s), so no restart is needed. An open position uses the new take profit and stop loss straight away; a new strategy only decides the next buy. Dry run, trade size and keys cannot be changed here: they live in .env on the server. A “sample” is one price reading: one per bot tick, about every 30 seconds.
